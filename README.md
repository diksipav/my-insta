I’m building an Instagram-like application with separate fe and be services as a hands-on learning project.

I’ve been studying system design, but many concepts are difficult to fully connect without building and operating something yourself. The goal is to gradually expand this project and make it increasingly production-ready while learning through practical decisions and trade-offs.

Planned areas include session-based authentication, PostgreSQL for persistent data, server-side rate limiting where appropriate (alongside protections provided by the hosting platform), media storage with Amazon S3 using multipart uploads and presigned URLs, etc.

I use AI too, ofc, but I try to remain responsible for the project’s design and implementation. I research concepts carefully and want to understand the reasoning behind my choices—although “thoroughly” may be optimistic, given that one concept can easily require a lifetime.

You can track my progress of learning new concepts at `my_progress.md`. I try to be very concise here and mention only important/bigger things.
